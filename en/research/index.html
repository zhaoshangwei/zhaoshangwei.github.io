Research / Areas of interest

Understanding systems.
Improving decisions.

System Modeling & Digital Twins

Combining physics-based understanding with data-driven models of complex industrial systems.

02 / CONTROL

Advanced Control & Process Optimization

Investigating model-predictive control, run-to-run methods and constrained optimization.

MPC · R2R · Robust Control

Scheduling & Operations Research

Using discrete-event simulation, combinatorial optimization and heuristics for capacity and planning decisions.

DES · Scheduling · OR
04 / LEARNING

Learning & Decision Intelligence

Connecting reinforcement learning with domain knowledge, optimization solvers and digital twins.

RL · Hybrid Methods
