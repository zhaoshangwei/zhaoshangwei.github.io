## The problem

Complex industrial processes can be coupled, nonlinear and subject to drift. Manual tuning is expensive in trial runs and may not consistently satisfy engineering constraints.

## Approach

Combine physical knowledge with process data to obtain interpretable and useful models. Optimization or learning methods then search for feasible parameter updates. Process feedback is used to correct predictions and support run-to-run adaptation.

## Technical focus

- Combining mechanistic, reduced-order and data-driven surrogate models
- Managing uncertainty and practical operating constraints
- Using feedforward context and downstream feedback together

## Public scope

The response trajectory is conceptual artwork, not a measured result. Process recipes, customer results and internal models are excluded.
