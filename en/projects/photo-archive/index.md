## Why I am building it

Photos and videos preserve memories, timestamps and context. I am exploring a personal system for long-term, faithful and controllable family media archiving without compromising usability.

## Product concept

A cross-platform experience organized around the time an event occurred. Originals and metadata are preserved, while lightweight previews are used when appropriate. Shared albums and private spaces have distinct permission boundaries.

## Technical exploration

- Flutter and consistent multi-device experiences
- Original-image and video preservation
- Batch management, timelines and album organization
- Authentication, permissions and private sharing

## Current status

An independent work in progress. This website does not access or expose any real private family media.
