## The problem

Manufacturing schedules balance delivery targets, resource utilization and shop-floor disturbances. Static plans may become infeasible after urgent orders, machine failures or processing delays.

## Approach

Express operation sequences, machine capacities, priorities and time windows in a consistent model. Constraint programming, mathematical optimization and heuristics generate feasible plans. Rolling-horizon rescheduling responds to meaningful changes.

## Research questions

- How should delivery, throughput and resource balance be traded off?
- When is local schedule repair preferable to a full re-optimization?
- How can simulation support the evaluation and explanation of schedules?

## Public scope

This is a general research and engineering theme, not a disclosure of a customer's production system or operational results.
